# Ideas

Origin: user V1 discussion, 2026-10-08. In-app audio recording/context extraction, optional account authentication, remote-network delivery, and more Aurora appearances remain possibilities. Files/images and arbitrary audio files are implemented with a 250 MiB/32-file aggregate limit; recording remains future scope. One-phone QR pairing and bidirectional text were selected and implemented; they are no longer speculative ideas.

Open choices: remote relay versus direct connection discovery, multi-device routing, larger attachment streaming/resume, auth provider and iOS share/background constraints. Android background receipt and instant share intake are implemented. No implementation permission or chosen solution is implied. A design board is optional and deferred; it is not selected roadmap work.

User feedback 2026-10-08 selected short flick sending, native background receipt, device naming and Direct Share visibility; these are implementation work, not future ideas. Sharing ranking remains Android-controlled. The subsequent user request selected files/images, selected-text Tunnel and per-type instant sharing; these are implemented. No following product task is selected.

2026-10-26: Five unselected launcher studies are in [logo-options.html](../design/logo-options.html): Nucleus (the in-app orbit, thickened), Bore (a clay tunnel mouth), Crossing (a color change as the wall), Rail (the send track), and Pair (phone and desk). A second pass the same day, after Nucleus and Crossing were preferred, adds Electron, Breach, and Field on the orbit, and Stride, Trace, and Rise on the wall. A third pass, after Stride, Rise, and Electron were the ones that held, adds Through, Risen, Mouth, and Lumen: the wall with the orbits, and the Bore mouth with the orbits and with Rise. A fourth pass, after Through was preferred and called too busy, adds Still, Axis, and Span: the same wall with the particle removed, then with two orbits, then with one ring. Axis was then selected and applied as the Windows and Android launcher. Its construction is [the Axis spec](../design/axis.html). The other studies stay unselected.

2026-10-31: three UI redesign directions are mocked in the private canvas [Tunnel UI Directions](https://claude.ai/artifact/7x3akERwF7fFfUYGNUZmwW): A Instrument (crisp light utility), B Dusk (mid-tone luminous dark with a bottom tab bar) and C Keys (tactile physical controls). Each keeps the send rail, large composer/Attach/Paste, destination chips, desktop sidebar and Recent column, and drops the editorial chrome. Dusk is the direction carried forward below. Instrument and Keys stay unselected.
