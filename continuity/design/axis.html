Logo specification

Axis

Accepted mark for October 26, 2026. Tide fills the left. Tide paper fills the right. Two orbits cross that wall, and the disc is reversed. Redraw other sizes from this page. Do not add a third orbit.

The mark

Square source art. The wall runs to the top and bottom edges so a circle or squircle can crop the corners.

Masks

The dashed circle is the Android safe zone. It is a guide. It is not part of the art. The orbits and the disc stay inside that circle. The wall does not.

Sizes

Keep the same 108 grid at every size. The stroke is 6.5 units on that grid. At 16 px the orbits are thin. Do not thicken them.

Color

Tide#376682
Tide paper#e4ebef
Mineral paper#f2f4ed
Ink#344640

Mineral paper is the orbit stroke on the Tide half. Ink is for the wordmark. It is not a color in the mark.

The only form

The full square is the logo. It is opaque, so the ground behind it does not show. There is no one-color substitute and no orbit-only mark.

Wordmark

Set Tunnel in Instrument Serif beside the square. Leave a gap about one third of the mark height.

Tunnel
Tunnel

Construction

Grid
108 by 108. Draw full bleed. Do not inset the wall.
Wall
Vertical split at x 54. Left is Tide. Right is Tide paper.
Orbits
Center 54, 54. One ellipse rx 26, ry 11. The second is that ellipse rotated 90° about the center.
Stroke
Width 6.5, no fill. Mineral paper on the left of x 54. Tide on the right. Clip at the wall.
Disc
Radius 7.5, centered, drawn over the crossing. Left half Tide paper. Right half Tide.
Safe circle
Diameter 66, centered. Radius 33. The stroke’s outer edge is 29.25 from the center.
Source
assets/tunnel.svg. The adaptive foreground is the same square.
